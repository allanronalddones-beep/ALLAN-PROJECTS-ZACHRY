Rotating Equipment General Reference

A working reference for pumps, compressors, turbines, expanders and the disciplines that surround them. Written for engineers doing selection, review and field work.

Offline by design. Every page in this reference is plain HTML with no external dependencies — no CDN, no fonts to download, no network calls. Copy the folder to a laptop or a USB stick and it works in full, including search, the unit converter and all equations. Any chapter prints cleanly to PDF from the browser.

Chapters

  • Chapter 1 Units, Conversions & Formulas Physical constants, full conversion tables, an interactive converter, and the working formulas for temperature, viscosity, SG/API, gas volumes, flow and head.
  • Chapter 2 Standards API, ISO, ASME, IEC and NEMA standards for rotating equipment, how the families map onto each other, and how to read a citation.
  • Chapter 3 Materials Material classes and temperature limits, properties, hardness and heat treatment, corrosion and sour service selection, and pressure–temperature ratings.
  • Chapter 4 Pump Selection API 610 type codes, affinity laws, specific speed, performance curves, NPSH, and vertical, sealless, positive displacement and cryogenic pumps.
  • Chapter 5 Compressor Selection Coverage by flow and pressure; fans and blowers, polytropic head and power, centrifugal surge control, reciprocating rod load, and screw compressors.
  • Chapter 6 Expander Selection Cryogenic turboexpanders and hot gas power recovery — expansion calculations, design problems and loading arrangements.
  • Chapter 7 Steam Turbine Selection Classification, exhaust and extraction arrangements, stage types, steam rate and efficiency, the Mollier diagram, governing and overspeed protection.
  • Chapter 8 Gas Turbine Selection Aeroderivative and frame machines, cycle performance, key performance data, site derating for ambient and altitude, fuel and emissions.
  • Chapter 9 Coupling Flexible element types, torque cases, service factors, spacer length and hub mounting, torsional considerations and API 671 requirements.
  • Chapter 10 Heat Exchanger TEMA designation and classes, LMTD and duty calculation, overall coefficients, fouling, air coolers, plate units and machine package coolers.
  • Chapter 11 Electrical Motors, cooling and mounting codes, insulation and efficiency, starting and testing, hazardous area classification, explosion protection, IP and NEMA ratings.
  • Chapter 12 Trade Terms Incoterms® 2020 — the eleven rules, where risk and cost transfer, insurance, and guidance for equipment procurement.
  • Chapter 13 Construction Installation sequence, foundations and grouting, balance quality grades, vibration criteria, shaft alignment and bolt tightening.
  • Chapter 14 Operation Pump and steam turbine start-up, running checks and shutdown, troubleshooting, and lubrication including oil mist, oilers and grease.

Provenance and scope

This reference is written independently, from publicly available standards and established engineering practice. It is not a reproduction or reformatting of any company’s internal handbook, and contains no third-party proprietary material, scanned publications, vendor catalogue extracts, vendor photographs or client project documents. The four figures are original SVG drawn for this reference.

Where a value or method derives from a published standard, that standard is cited by number and clause in the chapter’s Sources section. Standards themselves are not reproduced — a citation tells you where to look, and the standard remains the governing document.

Use with judgement. This is a quick-reference aid for experienced engineers, not a design code and not a substitute for the applicable standard, the vendor’s certified data, or project specifications. Verify anything that will be built, purchased or operated against the governing document. No warranty is given as to accuracy or fitness for any purpose.

How to use it

  • Search — press / anywhere to jump to the search box; arrow keys and Enter to navigate results.
  • Convert — the converter in §1.2.2 covers fourteen quantity types and shows the underlying factor for every pair.
  • Print — Ctrl+P on any chapter gives a clean document; navigation, search and the converter are suppressed automatically.
  • Share — the whole folder is self-contained. Zip it, copy it, or serve it from any static host.
